OUR STORY

A Passion for Exceptional Dining.

NOIR began as a small kitchen with a simple belief: great food starts with honest ingredients. Today our chefs work with local farms and trusted suppliers, building menus that follow the seasons.

Every plate is prepared by skilled hands and served by a team that treats each guest like family, so a night at NOIR feels both refined and relaxed.
